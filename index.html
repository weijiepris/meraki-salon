Since our humble beginnings

The Meraki story

From our humble beginnings in Tai Thong Crescent, where Meraki Salon SG first took shape as a small but passionate studio, we dedicated ourselves to perfecting our craft and building genuine relationships with every customer who walked through our doors.

As our reputation for thoughtful service and meticulous hair workmanship grew, so did our vision. This led us to relocate into the heart of Bugis, an energetic and vibrant district that allows us to welcome a wider community.

Here, we continue to share our expertise in creating bespoke hairstyles that enhance confidence, reflect individuality, and deliver an elevated salon experience rooted in care, artistry, and intention.

At Meraki Salon, excellence is something we work towards every day. We are grateful to have received the Goldwell Masterstylist Award, and our team continues to grow through professional training in London under the renowned Vidal Sassoon haircutting programme.

Before you come in

Good to know

How do I make an appointment?

WhatsApp is fastest — message us at +65 8855 0121 with your preferred date, time and the service you are after. You can also call the same number during opening hours, or email merakisalonsg@gmail.com. Walk-ins are welcome when we have a chair free, but weekends fill up quickly.

Do the promotion prices depend on my hair length?

The $268 single tone colour package covers all hair lengths, from short to extra-long. For other services, final pricing can vary with length, density and hair condition. Your stylist confirms the full price with you at consultation, before anything starts.

Which stylist should I book?

It depends on the look. For Korean air perms and anti-frizz work, ask for Nick or Vivi. For the Signature 3 Elements men style, Shady or Ken. For bobs and bold makeovers, Calvin. Grey hair blending is handled by Vivi, Ken and Calvin. If you are unsure, send a reference photo on WhatsApp and we will match you.

My hair is bleached or damaged. Can I still perm or colour it?

Sometimes the honest answer is not yet. We assess your hair condition before any chemical service and will recommend waiting or doing a repair treatment first if that gives you a better long-term result. We would rather you come back with healthy hair than push a service that will not hold.

How long do results last?

It varies by service and hair type, and your stylist will give you a realistic timeframe on the day. Anti-frizz treatments are designed for long-lasting manageability in Singapore's humidity, and our perms are cut and shaped so they grow out seamlessly rather than dropping suddenly. Home care makes a real difference — we will show you exactly how to style and maintain it.
